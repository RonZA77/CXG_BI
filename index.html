Key Observations · May 2026
  • Monthusetsi Nakedi carries the highest weighted workload — 116 instances / ~119h per month. Her 4 daily reports (KFC, MVNE) alone contribute 80 weighted instances, making her the most active deliver in the team despite holding only 22 unique reports.
  • Akhona Mlobeli holds the most unique reports (40) at 94 weighted instances / ~91h. Her portfolio is weighted heavily toward weekly cadence (18 reports × 4 = 72 instances) and requires significant ongoing coordination across Famous Brands, Nando's and KFC.
  • Ronald Maswanganyi has increased slightly to 23 unique reports (41 weighted / ~53.5h), now including the Ackermans CX and Escalation reports previously held by Ronald and reabsorbed in the updated list. His portfolio is predominantly monthly.
  • Brit, Alex/Kwena and Lilly Magoro collectively hold 33 unique reports (58 weighted) — largely monthly and weekly cadences. Brit's Spur Group book (8 reports) and Lilly's Pepkor set represent the most concentrated brand exposures for these analysts.
  • Dumi's portfolio has been significantly reduced to just 2 monthly reports (Ackermans MBR and Connect), down from 8 in the prior version. This may reflect a transitional state or a capacity adjustment requiring follow-up.

Conclusion
Portfolio Conclusion · May 2026
  • The top 5 brands account for 96 of 120 reports (80% of unique volume) — Famous Brands, Nando's, KFC, Pepkor and Spur. These accounts drive the majority of team capacity and should be prioritised in cover planning and succession arrangements.
  • KFC is the most operationally intensive brand per report, with 4 daily and 6 weekly cadences. Despite 15 unique reports, it generates 67 weighted instances per month — more than the entire Spur portfolio times seven.
  • Dumi's portfolio has been reduced to 2 reports (Ackermans MBR and Connect). This is a significant step down from prior versions and may indicate a capacity or role change that warrants clarification with the line manager.
  • 16 brands are served by single reports, representing valuable but relatively low-effort client relationships. Ensuring these remain current and that ownership is clearly documented is important given the ongoing team restructuring.
